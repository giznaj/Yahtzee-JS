Yahtzee Rules & Scoring Guide

How to Play:

  • You have 13 turns per game (one for each scoring category).
  • On each turn, you can roll the dice up to 3 times.
  • Click any die to Hold it between rolls so it won't be re-rolled.
  • After rolling, select an open category from the table or dropdown and click Save Score.
  • If your roll doesn't fit any available category, you can choose a category and click Take Zero (Scratch).

Upper Section Scoring:

Score the sum of the dice matching that category number (e.g. three 4s in Fours = 12 pts). If your Upper Section subtotal reaches 63 or more, you earn a 35 point bonus!

Lower Section Scoring:
